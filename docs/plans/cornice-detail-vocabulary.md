Reuse those dimensions and the accepted material/seed/source pipeline; add no preassembled component mesh or provider request.

The two detailed modes carve a bounded band of that continuous solid into backing and repeated units. Keep every new unit and backing section within the original cornice envelope. The moulded mode remains literal. Dentils use small closed bevelled blocks; brackets use closed parametric profiles. Keep original coping, parapet, roof deck, windows, wall and door geometry exact. This changes visible depth and negative space without pushing decorative geometry into previously clear building space.

Cadence is derived deterministically from physical span and existing cornice dimensions, with balanced runs and explicit corner clearance on all four faces. Reject non-fitting or over-budget requests rather than silently clamp them. Bound detail units at512 before allocating geometry. Give each unit a stable face/ordinal planning address; do not serialize renderer indices or add local detail selection in this packet. All faces, including ends, require finite outward geometry and physical UVs. Avoid exposed same-facing coplanar surfaces; closed internal contacts are not a texture workaround.

Use the original `trim.horizontal.primary` role and its existing isolated metre-tile/compositor path. Structural treatment/height/projection changes retain all material artifacts and source provenance. No new density, seed or material panel is needed for the first version.
