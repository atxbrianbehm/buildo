# Authored street segment: sequential implementation plan

**Status:** preparation only, 2026-10-29. The [Phase F gate](kit-grammar-wfc-utdg-block.md#12-visual-qa-checklist-gate-between-phases) remains in force: checklist items 1–7 must pass for kit mode on a representative machine, with a stored visual QA packet, before Phase F implementation starts. The accepted six-building family direction is useful evidence but does not establish the whole gate. The current workstation measurements are local results, not a representative-machine claim.

## Product outcome

An artist assembles a street segment of 4–12 related but independently editable buildings along a curb, with explicit lot frontage, depth and spacing, varied heights, and at least one corner treatment. A saved segment must reopen with its placements, building identities, authored edits, generation lineage and shared material source intact. The segment should remain deterministic for a given admitted source and seed; the controls must expose architectural choices without requiring graph editing. Existing advanced graph access remains available.

This is a bounded street segment, not a city simulation. UTDG can supply verified source-backed priors and provenance when available; demo material tags do not establish historical architecture coverage. Geometry remains procedural, and structure edits must not regenerate material artifacts.

## Starting seams and constraints

- `contracts/constructionFamilyPlan.ts` and `state/constructionFamilySource.ts` deliberately admit exactly six family members. The latter owns the original building, six member graphs and lineages, and one material source. Existing family files and their validation must retain their meaning; a segment cannot silently reinterpret that format as an arbitrary-size street.
- `ui/bayConstructionFixture.ts` positions six buildings in a fixed, evenly spaced comparison row. It is not a parcel plan, collision check, curb layout, or corner building.
- `ui/BayConstructionWorkbench.tsx` and family-member editing already supply the original and independently owned member sources. Preserve targeted opening identities, edits, save/open behavior and failure retention when adapting those seams; in-session history is not currently serialized.
- `renderer-three/familyRuntime.ts` owns a family group, member runtimes and shared resources. Reuse its lifecycle principles and the bounded preparation cache where applicable, while measuring actual ownership and disposal for the new segment. Sharing a material source does not automatically mean every member shares all runtime textures.
- The proposed `ParcelPlan` and Phase F1/F2 acceptance in [the kit roadmap](kit-grammar-wfc-utdg-block.md#11-phase-f--block-of-buildings) remain authoritative. Resolve its illustrative shape into a validated owned contract during the first implementation packet, rather than treating the example as an existing implementation.

## Packets after the gate

Execute one packet at a time and publish each as a separate stacked PR. The later packets depend on the earlier ones; passing a source test alone does not imply a rendered or artist-accepted street.

| Packet | Scope and result | Verification required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R7.P0 — parcel source and deterministic plan** | Create a strict, schema-versioned segment request and plan for 4–12 lots. Define stable segment/lot identities, seeded building inputs, frontage, footprint, spacing and explicit corner intent; reject unsupported or geometrically inconsistent inputs rather than inventing fallback buildings. Keep the existing six-member format unchanged. | Deterministic replay and semantic seed separation; 4/12 boundaries and invalid counts; distinct stable identities; finite dimensions, placement and overlap/spacing constraints; corner and frontage cases; malformed/version/tamper rejection and input ownership; focused tests, typecheck and lint. | **R7.P4 — visual review and performance evidence** | Present whole segment, corner, close material/geometry interactions and selected-building edits in the existing phone gallery. Each packet must retain evidence and its own clear completion status in the revival plan. If the representative-device or checklist gate is still open, preparation and unrelated work may proceed, but R7.P0 implementation does not start.
