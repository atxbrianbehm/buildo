# Phase F visual gate: existing evidence index

This index organizes existing review captures; it is not a new pass or a representative-machine QA packet. The C10 single-building captures and R6 family captures were made on the current high-end workstation on different runs. They predate the R6 surface-reuse optimization, whose tested family pixels were unchanged on both backends. The [roadmap](../plans/kit-grammar-wfc-utdg-block.md#12-visual-qa-checklist-gate-between-phases) requires items 1–7 to pass on a representative machine with a stored visual QA packet before Phase F. The user's existing approvals accept the specific whole-building, recipe, cornice, parapet and family directions; they do not independently establish that entire checklist or choose a representative machine. The [portable family replay](2026-10-29-r6-portable-review/README.md) prepares comparable device evidence, but its overview screenshots do not supply all visual angles. A representative-device packet must record its source, seeds, hardware, backend, actual views and explicit review outcomes; the shadow issue must remain visible in that assessment.
