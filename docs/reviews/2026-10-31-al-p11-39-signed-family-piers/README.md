# AL.P11.39 — facade-pier variation on signed buildings

The isolated retained-sign family study can apply the existing seeded demo pier policy: none, slender or projecting. It preserves the source sign, awning, supports, clearance and material inputs; already-authored piers remain unchanged. A version 0.3 receipt records the pier policy hash along with the composition and final signed-geometry hashes. Prior receipt versions remain unchanged when no pier policy is supplied.

The [family view](family.png) shows a more pronounced facade rhythm, and the [closer view](pier-detail.png) shows the masonry and trim next to the signed storefront. The result still repeats its brick, storefront and window treatment; the close view exposes visible trim ends at the side and is not a complete texture or silhouette review. This is a demo study, not a historical claim, saved grouped-family policy, artist control or street-ready baseline.
