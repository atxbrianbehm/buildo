# AL.P11.23 — retained sign family study

An isolated, roof-only family study compiles six members while retaining an authored sign, awning, supports, gap and pinned material lineage. It re-evaluates the final signed geometry, validates worker protocol shape and emits a versioned receipt with distinct final-geometry hashes. Unsupported variation groups, release of authored choices, imported sign-related locks and cancellation are guarded.

The WebGL textured family fixture shows six geometrically distinct members, but their shared massing, openings, facade, storefront and materials make them read as near-copies. This is **not sufficient visual diversity for the user's street goal**, and the hashes must not be treated as visual acceptance. Wider massing and architectural variation, family persistence and workspace integration remain pending. The image is an illustrative demo, not historical evidence; architecture coverage remains unknown.
