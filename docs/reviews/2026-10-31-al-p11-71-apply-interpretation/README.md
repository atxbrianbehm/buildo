# AL.P11.71: Apply an interpretation in the workbench

An artist can explicitly apply a proposed flat or round upper-front opening head from the research inspector. The workbench checks the accepted graph, current opening lock and generation-source pins, compiles the result, and records the application with the corresponding undo state. Undo and redo restore the matching geometry and application. A new edit creates an unlinked history state; the save button and notice say when the earlier interpretation will not be included.

Saving an applied state writes a `construction-evidence-project` snapshot, including the complete staged extract, source claim, application, building and material source. Opening that file validates and replays the record before displaying it. The interface discloses that the entire extract is embedded. Invalid snapshots retain the current building. The claim stays unreviewed and its producer and historical accuracy are not authenticated. Family previews and family files do not yet carry the application record; the workbench tells the artist to save the linked building separately.

The synthetic app workflow exercises an opening lock, explicit apply, geometry change, linked save, undo, redo, reopen, invalid-file retention, desktop layout and phone width. It checks that the material provider is not called. Reproduce with `node docs/reviews/2026-10-31-al-p11-71-apply-interpretation/verify.mjs webgl <new-output-dir>` or `webgpu`. Captures are in the Buildo phone review gallery; the images do not establish historical evidence or broader street readiness.

Validation passed: typecheck, lint, production build, focused interaction/history tests, full suite with two workers (2,155 tests in 190 files), standard browser smoke, both renderer workflows at desktop and phone sizes, and the Impeccable detector.
