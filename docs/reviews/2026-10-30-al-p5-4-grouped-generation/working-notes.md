# Grouped generation working notes

First remove the temporary generation guard safely: carry authored group directives when either “Keep edited dimensions and assemblies” or “Lock openings” is set. When both are explicitly released, the existing generation semantics may release them. Recompute placements from the newly resolved graph; if a target floor/bay disappears, a group no longer fits, or the generated head is incompatible, reject the candidate and retain the accepted source rather than silently dropping or clamping a directive. Preserve untouched graph and material lineage. A separately selected family composition that asks for round-head variation must not silently replace or invalidate a retained grouped bay.

Then add family-level grouping variation as a separately versioned opt-in policy. Its choices must be visible, seeded and repeatable, respect opening locks and authored group overrides, and preserve prior family source versions. Do not label a bounded demo catalog as historical evidence. Prove both independent member editing and six-member family save/open on WebGPU and WebGL, with unchanged material artifacts, and inspect actual rendered families. This note records the original design direction; the packet README states what was implemented and verified.
