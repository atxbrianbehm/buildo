# AL.P11.29 — explicit grouped-facade rhythm study

This isolated compiler study repeats paired or triple rectangular openings across an explicit inclusive range of upper floors and front bays in a signed storefront graph. It rejects invalid extents, existing grouped-bay or local-width overlaps, round heads and physical fit failures, and compiles the complete result before returning it. The input graph remains owned by its caller; the sign, awning and clearance are retained.

The [textured family render](family.png) applies paired openings across the upper facades of flat-headed members; arched members remain unchanged. The larger rhythm is visibly clearer than a single grouped bay. Storefronts, signage and brick still repeat, so it remains a demo rather than a street-ready or historical baseline. This graph-only study has no lineage or evidence-lock input and is not connected to generation, the editor or saved families; any integration must explicitly enforce imported locks and authored precedence before applying the rhythm.

Typecheck, lint, build, browser smoke and the full suite passed (2,121 tests in 180 files). The build retains its existing chunk-size warning.
