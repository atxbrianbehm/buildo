The family view now lets the artist choose one of the six buildings and open it in the existing construction editor. The member has its own in-session undo/redo history. Returning recompiles the family view; a dirty member draft must be applied or reverted first, and edited family changes must be saved before returning to the original building or opening another family. The family file retains each member graph and the shared material source. Member histories are in-session and are not serialized in the family file.
