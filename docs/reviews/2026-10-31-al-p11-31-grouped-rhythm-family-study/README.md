# AL.P11.31 — grouped rhythm family study

Round-headed members are left unchanged. The policy and receipt are not yet persisted in an editable family project or exposed in artist controls. Storefronts and brick remain repetitive; architecture coverage is unknown and the image is not a street-ready or historical baseline.
