# T7 — shadow-frustum quality study

A tighter shadow projection is a promising, bounded candidate for the fringed edge without increasing the4096×4096 map or changing bias. In the reviewed building, the current symmetric half-extent is14.028 m. The full construction bounds project to a required11.058 m at the default light and9.330 m at the lower light. A10% margin gives12.2 m and10.3 m respectively. All eight projected bounds corners fit each tested candidate, but this alone does not prove clipping safety for other scenes or light angles.

On actual WebGPU, eight temporary frustum candidates at two light angles restored their native pixels and camera exactly. A second WebGPU run compared the10% margin at five real views: whole/default, close/default, close/lower light, whole/lower light and sill/default. Every original frame and camera restored exactly; source geometry, material channels, finish bindings and the original light settings stayed exact. The diagnostic consumes residual orbit damping before each fixed-camera comparison and restores the damping setting; it does not claim that camera setup was entirely unmodified. [Sweep](webgpu-02/receipt.json), [five-view coverage](coverage-webgpu-01/receipt.json).

WebGL's live frustum restore exposed the prior one-channel variance: two lower-light attempts differed by one channel in one and three pixels, despite exact camera and light matrices. These attempts remain [excluded](excluded/) and are not represented as exact restorations. In independently initialized WebGL renderers, the default14.028 m and a static12.2 m diagnostic projection were captured with identical source and settings. Whole/default, whole/lower and sill/default have exact camera, light, compiled geometry and bindings across both runs; independently interacted close cameras differed and are excluded from pixel scoring. The static candidate is an ephemeral source transform used only for this experiment, not a product change. [Fresh comparison](fresh-webgl-comparison.json).

An8192×8192 map at the original projection supplies a finite-resolution reference, not physical ground truth and not a proposed shipping setting. For pixels where either4096 result differs from the reference by more than one mean sRGB channel value, the tighter projection's mean absolute channel error is3.98 versus4.85 at whole/default;3.53 versus4.18 at whole/lower; and4.03 versus5.56 at the sill. On the sill,16,904 scored pixels are closer to the reference with the tighter projection and6,626 are closer with the original. This supports further investigation, not a claim that every edge is improved. [Method and counts](reference-comparison.json); [reproduction script](reference-metrics.py).

No product code or material source changes; all354 earlier code/config/test pins remain exact, and no material/provider generation occurred during the comparisons. The high-resolution run uses four times as many shadow-map pixels and is a reference only. Multiple footprints, heights, stage states, light directions, caster/ground coverage, renderer lifecycle and representative performance remain unproven. The existing C9 visual decision and separate lighting review remain pending.
