# C10.P1 — procedural stepped parapet geometry

The existing `BuildingRoof` compiler now accepts an explicit graph/worker 0.9 silhouette choice. The raised form adds a closed front-center solid and sweeps the coping profile around the roof and up and over both mitered steps. The original flat geometry remains the default and is byte-identical in all three covered cornice treatments. The asymmetric geometry is pre-mirrored at the existing global-mesh seam so it lands on the front, with a regression test for its actual emitted positions.

Sixty-eight focused tests in five files, typecheck and lint pass. Coverage checks finite/outward, geometrically closed triangles and metre-scale UVs at three dimensional extremes; actual compiler results for small and tall buildings; flat buffers; all three accepted cornice backing and repeat positions; other mesh buffers, instances and prototypes; storefront, window and selected-opening edits; and graph/worker protocol admission and client ownership. The added solid and lower parapet meet at an internal contact. This does not establish every arbitrary source or a textured runtime appearance.

The [flat](geometry-preview-02/flat.png) and [raised](geometry-preview-02/raised.png) pictures are a separate local clay diagnostic built from actual compiler IR and procedural prototypes. They are not captures of the product workbench, do not use its textures, and use automatically fitted cameras that differ between the two forms. The first diagnostic attempt failed because its harness assumed all instance roles were present; the corrected harness supplied a default. An intermediate test also assumed IR batches had bounds, then was corrected to measure emitted vertices. Neither failure was used as passing evidence.
