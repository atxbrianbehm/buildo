# AL.P9.2d: Awning family retention and rendering

The family workspace retains authored awning directives and one independently validated fabric source through six-member generation, shared scene preparation, individual member editing and family save/reopen. Family members share one fabric material owner; scene leases keep it alive and dispose it after the family closes. Existing family documents without a fabric source cannot introduce one solely in a member; the control explains that the original building must receive an awning before creating that family. Existing source and atlas remain pinned.

The workflow removes one member's awning, verifies siblings and original remain unchanged, saves and reopens the family, and observes no new material-provider calls. [Member edit](webgl-member.png) shows the selected building without that awning. Focused tests exercise source replay, an existing roof composition policy with retained awnings, shared material identity and one-time disposal. These are related buildings but the same awning repeats and broader facade variety remains limited; this is not street-ready or historical evidence.
