# AL.P11.32 — portable grouped-rhythm family study

This experimental, runtime-validated archive carries a signed source project with its real material and awning-fabric sources, the family plan and composition, explicit rhythm policy and controls, final member graphs and generation lineage, and the final-geometry receipt. Reading verifies the archive hash and source project, then replays the complete family and compares its saved members and receipt. It rejects changed results even if the outer hash is recomputed.

The archive is a programmatic study format; it is not yet the app's editable family-project format, an automatic checkpoint, or an artist-facing save/open workflow. The [prior family-level render](../2026-10-31-al-p11-31-grouped-rhythm-family-study/family.png) shows the geometry being archived, with repeated storefronts and material still limiting diversity. No historical architectural coverage is claimed.

Typecheck, lint, build, browser smoke and the full suite passed (2,124 tests in 181 files). The build retains its existing chunk-size warning.
