# C10.P2 — parapet authoring and continuity

Roof & trim now offers Flat parapet and Raised center on the existing construction graph. Graph/worker 0.9 and portable project 0.10 preserve the selection with authored dimensions, cornice, window, frontage, local-width and semantic-surface choices. Regeneration retains the roof choices when authored edits are kept; opening locks alone do not lock the roof. The generation source and recipe remain literal evidence rather than being relabeled as a historical style.

Textured workbench inspection of the P1 two-solid candidate exposed a brick-phase discontinuity and a faint horizontal line even with texture maps disabled. P2 replaces the touching parapet solids with one closed boundary mesh and maps the front masonry continuously in metre coordinates. The corrected WebGPU and WebGL close views show continuous brick courses across the former join; map-disabled views no longer show the observed contact line. The before and after close cameras are not exactly equal, so this is a visual observation supported by the closed-boundary and UV tests, not an exact pixel comparison. Existing shadow fringe remains visible.

Each native backend exercised seven recorded states and ten captures, including actual flat/raised views, texture-map controls, undo/redo, retained and released regeneration, saved-source reopening, invalid-draft retention, room return, cold recovery, and compact keyboard focus. Channels, finish bindings and unaffected geometry were preserved; no extra provider or material generation occurred after source open, and cold recovery made zero such calls. The final reset, room-return and cold frames and cameras match within each successful run. Both runs retained 359 source/config/test pins. WebGL emitted the known soft-PCF fallback warning. Initial WebGPU attempt 01 is excluded for a strict cold camera difference of approximately 3e-14; attempt 02 is preserved as the pre-repair join evidence; attempt 03 verifies the corrected source before expanded room/keyboard checks.

Saved portable files are archived as content-addressed gzip streams. [The manifest](portable/manifest.json) maps each backend/file alias to its raw SHA-256 and byte count; decompression was checked against the original bytes. Native scripts are stored as `.mjs.txt` snapshots. The close-up diagnostic temporarily disables texture maps on cloned materials and restores them; it is not a product control. The earlier failed and superseded attempts are retained under `excluded/`.

An additional native run on each backend changes parapet height, checks that bounds change while channels and bindings remain exact, captures front, side and rear views, and verifies Undo. Light triangular gaps in the ground shadow are visible from the rear and side. They also appear in a similar WebGPU flat-baseline view; the cameras differ and their cause has not been established. They remain an open visual issue, not a claimed parapet-specific regression.

All five repository checks pass: typecheck, lint, 1,948 tests in 135 files, build and eight E2E workflows. The build retains its large-chunk warning. The final 339 source/config pins and each native run's 359 pins match at verification. See [verification](verification.json) and the retained logs.

This is one additional geometric treatment of the existing rectangular family, not a sourced historical UTDG style, an artist-study result, or a block/performance claim. Human visual review remains separate.
