# Storefront composition working notes

The reference streets contain distinct storefront rhythms, not just one undivided display and one transom per bay. The existing `PlaceStorefront` plan already creates real ground-floor apertures, a transom rail, a glazed entrance and individual display scopes; `buildConstructionStorefrontGeometry` currently emits a single lower display pane and a single transom pane. A useful next vocabulary slice is a display divided into one or two lower lights and a transom divided into one, two or three lights, with actual timber mullions and separate glass panes. Entrance transoms can use the same transom divisions while preserving the door leaf and hardware.

Plan the clear rectangles and mullion widths from the admitted storefront plan, including minimum clear sizes, local coordinates, depth and exact fit. Preserve wall apertures, door identity, transom rail, bulkhead and material slots; do not fake divisions with texture or a preassembled mesh. A versioned, deterministic plan should feed the geometry, followed by a separate graph/worker and artist-control packet with legacy save/open compatibility. When combined with seeded family composition, choices and scope must be explicit and must not overwrite locks or authored storefronts.

The supplied references are visual direction, not measured or verified historical data. Divided glazing alone does not cover recessed entrances, varied storefront widths, signs, awnings, massing or street readiness. The isolated planner and geometry now exist; graph, worker, artist controls and textured app review remain subsequent work.
